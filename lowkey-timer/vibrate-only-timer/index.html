How to Set a Vibrate-Only Timer on iPhone

Short answer: the iPhone's built-in timer can't vibrate without making a sound. When it ends, it plays a tone, or nothing at all if you pick Stop Playing. Here are your options.

Option 1: Use an alarm instead

Built-in alarms can vibrate only. Set an alarm for the time you want, tap Sound, choose None, and make sure a vibration is selected. This works for one-off reminders, but you have to work out the end time yourself, and there's no countdown.

Option 2: Use a silent ringtone

Some people make a silent ringtone (for example in GarageBand) and choose it as the timer sound, so the phone vibrates without playing anything. It works, but it takes some setup, and the silent tone shows up in every sound list on your phone.
