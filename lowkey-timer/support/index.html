LowKey Timer Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email lowkey@eyelidsup.com.

My timer or alarm didn't go off

LowKey Timer needs permission to use alarms. Open the Settings app, find LowKey Timer, and make sure Alarms is turned on.

How do I make a timer vibrate only?

When setting up a timer or alarm, tap When Timer Ends (or When Alarm Goes Off) and choose Vibrate Only.

How does the free trial work?

Everything is free for 7 days. The trial doesn't renew or charge you. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks the app for good. There's no subscription. Timers and alarms you've already set keep working either way.

I bought the unlock but the app doesn't show it

Open Settings in the app (the gear at the top right) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
