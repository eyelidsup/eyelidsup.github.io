LowKey Timer Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

LowKey Timer doesn't collect, store, or share any of your personal information. There's no account to create, and the app doesn't use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your iPhone

Your timers, alarms, presets, stopwatches, and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They're never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Alarms and notifications

The app asks for permission to schedule alarms so your timers and alarms can alert you on the lock screen. iOS handles these alerts on your device. Nothing about them leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account. The app only learns from Apple whether the trial or unlock is active on your device. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email lowkey@eyelidsup.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
